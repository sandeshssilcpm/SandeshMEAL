About this workbook & scoring notes (from the first sheet)

Purpose: Record teacher/SMC and student questionnaire scores at baseline and endline for up to 20 schools, using one identical layout, and compare change within and across schools.

How it is linked: School_List (names) → S01…S20 (one entry sheet per school) → Compare_All (all schools side by side) → Change_Heatmap (change only, with ranking). Everything after the entry sheets is formula-driven; never type over it.

Step 1: On School_List, type each school's name, district and survey dates. Names flow automatically to every other sheet.

Step 2: Open a school sheet (S01–S20, or click 'Open' on School_List). Enter baseline scores in the BLUE cells and endline scores in the ORANGE cells. S01 is already filled with the Shree Yogani baseline as a worked example.

Step 3: Read the school's own summary at the top of its sheet. Then open Compare_All and Change_Heatmap to compare schools.

Colour key: Blue cells = baseline input. Orange cells = endline input. Green headers/grey-green rows = automatic results. Green/red shading on change cells = increase/decrease.

Scoring scale: Type scores as a percentage: 62% or 62 or 0.62 all give 62%. Cells reject anything outside 0–100%. Use the same questions, scoring rules and calculation method at endline as at baseline.

Change (pp): Change = endline − baseline, shown in percentage points (pp). Example: 60% − 50% = +10 pp. This differs from a % change (+20%), which is not used here.

Capacity per school: Up to 10 teacher/SMC respondents and 20 students per school. Insert rows inside the blocks if you ever need more (formulas expand automatically), or tell me and I will extend the template.

Student overall: 'Student – overall' is a simple unweighted average of the five student domain averages. It appears only when all five domains have a score. It is a convenience figure for ranking, not a validated index.

Network averages: The network summary on Compare_All is like-for-like: it only averages schools that have BOTH baseline and endline for that indicator, so incomplete schools do not distort the change. Each school counts equally regardless of how many students were surveyed.

Teacher/SMC vs students: Teacher/SMC and student scores are kept separate because the questionnaires capture different perspectives. Do not combine them. Only total teacher/SMC scores are held (no domain-level scores).

Sampling caution: The Shree Yogani baseline has only five student respondents out of 367 students, so it is not representative of the whole school. Check each school's respondent counts on School_List. Where feasible revisit the same respondents at endline; otherwise record the change in sampling in the notes box on the school sheet.

Averages vs matched change: School averages compare the group at baseline with the group at endline. They equal the average of matched-respondent changes only if the same respondents are scored at both rounds. The per-respondent change columns are meaningful only when IDs are matched.

Interpretation: A positive change means a higher score. A score alone does not explain why change happened: read alongside field observations and qualitative feedback.

Recce data: Sheet 'Minor School Ext 26-27 Recce' holds the Minor School Extension FY2026/27 recce report (location, structures, WASH proposals, costs). School ID links it to School_List and S01–S20; key recce figures are pulled into School_List columns N–V.
